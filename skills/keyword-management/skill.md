# Keyword Strategist

A skill for transforming raw search terms into high-converting SEO and PPC assets using intent-driven clustering and entity optimization.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Intent & Entity Discovery
Identify the core entities (people, places, concepts) rather than just strings.
*   **Search Intent Check:** Categorize every term into Informational, Navigational, Commercial, or Transactional.
*   **Conversational Mapping:** Rephrase short-tail keywords into natural language questions (e.g., "fasting schedule" -> "What is the best intermittent fasting schedule for a busy professional?").
*   **AI Overview Targeting:** Identify complex, nuanced queries that require synthesized answers, positioning content for Google’s AI summaries.

### Phase 2: Topic Clustering (Hub & Spoke)
Organize keywords into clusters to build topical authority.
*   **The Hub (Pillar):** A high-volume, broad term.
*   **The Spokes (Cluster):** Long-tail, specific queries that link back to the hub.
*   **Zero-Search Volume Logic:** Include hyper-niche terms that tools might label "0 volume" but possess high conversion intent for specific ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles).

### Phase 3: Channel Mapping
*   **SEO:** Map informational/commercial long-tails to blog posts and guides.
*   **PPC:** Map transactional/commercial terms to landing pages and specific ad groups. Use negative keyword suggestions to prune irrelevant traffic.

## Output Structure
ALWAYS provide data in an organized table:

| Keyword/Phrase | Intent | Match Type | Cluster/Theme | Content Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Term] | [e.g., Transactional] | [PPC Only] | [Topic] | [e.g., Landing Page] |

## Optimization Guidelines
*   **Entity SEO:** Ensure the content covers related entities (Latent Semantic Indexing) to provide context to search engines.
*   **Readability:** Maintain a clear, minimalist structure in the suggested content to keep engagement high (crucial for 2026 ranking factors).
